As a student at the University of Michigan, I have had the opportunity to explore various cultures and traditions. However, my experience in Dublin has been truly unique and eye-opening. From the moment I arrived, I was captivated by the rich history and vibrant culture that permeates every corner of this city. This page is specifically dedicated to my side quests in the country.

Adventures on the Island

One of the most exciting aspects of the trip was going to see what makes Ireland so precious. This took me directly to St. Patruck's Cathedral, which is one of the most iconic landmarks in Dublin. The cathedral is a stunning example of Gothic architecture and is steeped in history. I was amazed by the intricate details of the building and the beautiful stained glass windows. It was a humbling experience to be in such a historic place and to learn about the rich cultural heritage of Ireland.

I've learned to value identity and culture, especially considereing where I've come from and the things that I've seen. I think's it's beautiful to think that on a single day of the year, people all around the world come together to celebrate their Irish heritage and culture. It's a testament to the enduring power of culture and tradition, and it's something that I will always cherish about my time in Dublin. I understand why "On March 17th, everyone is Irish."

Galway

Another highlight of my trip was visiting Galway, a charming city located on the west coast of Ireland. Galway is known for its vibrant arts scene, colorful streets, and lively atmosphere. I spent a day exploring the city and was blown away by the beauty and energy of the place. The streets were filled with musicians, artists, and performers, creating a lively and festive atmosphere. I also had the opportunity to try some traditional Irish food and drink, which was delicious. Overall, my visit to Galway was a memorable experience that allowed me to immerse myself in Irish culture and connect with the local community.

Galway was such an unexpected treasure. I had no idea what to expect when I arrived, but I was immediately charmed by the city's unique character and vibrant culture. The people were amazing, the food was great, and my absolute favorite part was going to see the Cliffs of Moher. The cliffs were absolutely breathtaking, with stunning views of the Atlantic Ocean and the rugged coastline. It was my first time seein the Atlantic Ocean from this side and I was absolutely blown away.

Guinness Storehouse

One of the most iconic symbols of Ireland is Guinness, and I had the opportunity to visit the Guinness Storehouse during my trip. The storehouse is a seven-story building that is also the worlds largest pint glass. It offers a fascinating look at the history and production of Guinness, as well as stunning views of Dublin from the rooftop bar. Beer isn't really my thing, but I found the experience to be really interesting and informative and discovered that Dublin's History Is that rich because Guinnesses History is that rich.

Retail

Finally, I had the opportunity to explore the retail scene in Dublin, which was a unique and enjoyable experience. Dublin has a vibrant retail scene, with a mix of high-end that you'd typically find in a big city and charming local shops that you wouldn't expect to find anywhere else. I just wanted to take some time to depict some of my favorite store that made Dublin feel like home.
