As a student at the University of Michigan, I have had the opportunity to explore various cultures and traditions. However, my experience in Dublin has been truly unique and eye-opening. From the moment I arrived, I was captivated by the rich history and vibrant culture that permeates every corner of this city. This page is specifically dedicated to my side quests in the country.

Adventures on the Island

One of the most exciting aspects of the trip was going to see what makes Ireland so precious. This took me directly to St. Patruck's Cathedral, which is one of the most iconic landmarks in Dublin. The cathedral is a stunning example of Gothic architecture and is steeped in history. I was amazed by the intricate details of the building and the beautiful stained glass windows. It was a humbling experience to be in such a historic place and to learn about the rich cultural heritage of Ireland.

I've learned to value identity and culture, especially considereing where I've come from and the things that I've seen. I think's it's beautiful to think that on a single day of the year, people all around the world come together to celebrate their Irish heritage and culture. It's a testament to the enduring power of culture and tradition, and it's something that I will always cherish about my time in Dublin. I understand why "On March 17th, everyone is Irish."
